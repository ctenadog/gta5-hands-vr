# 0.6.6 - virtual steering wheel

- In a vehicle: hold both controllers in front of you like a steering wheel and turn them - the car steers.
  The tilt of the line between the hands (room space, independent of where you look) is the wheel angle:
  right hand lower = right. Full lock at 90 deg (GTA5VR.ini wheel_angle), 4 deg deadzone, sent as INPUT_VEH_MOVE_LR.
- Active only while the hands are 20-90 cm apart and at least 12 cm in front of the head; otherwise (hands on the lap,
  one controller lost) nothing is sent. The left stick still steers and overrides the wheel while it is pushed.
- GTA5VR.ini [vr] wheel=0 turns it off. Log: "wheel: hands on/off the wheel", "wheel: angle X deg -> steer Y" every 2 s.
- Untested in game.
